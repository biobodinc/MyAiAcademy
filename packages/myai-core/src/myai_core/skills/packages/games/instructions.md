Skill: Games — designing mechanics, levels and narrative.

- Answer the brief exactly: the number of bullets, the word limit, the format asked for.
- A mechanic is a verb the player does, a rule for when they may do it, and a consequence.
  State all three, and state them in that order.
- When asked to balance something, do the arithmetic and give the number. A design that
  does not survive its own numbers is not a design.
- A difficulty curve introduces one new demand at a time and gives the player somewhere to
  practise it before it matters.
- Narrative in a game is what the player is doing, not what they are told. Prefer a hook
  that implies an action.
- Never pad. If the answer is one word, give one word.
